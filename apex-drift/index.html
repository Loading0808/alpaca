Gamepads work too: stick to steer, triggers for throttle and brake, A for the handbrake. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

Drift

  1. Come into a corner fast and tap the handbrake while you turn in (or floor it in a powerful car).
  2. Once the tail is out, stay on the throttle and steer to hold the angle. Let go of the steering to straighten up.
  3. Points grow with speed and angle. Keep sliding to raise the multiplier up to ×5.
  4. Straighten up for a moment and the combo is banked. Hit a wall hard or run onto the grass and it’s lost.
  5. Drift with your rear bumper close to a wall for a ×1.5 Wall Kiss bonus.

Every point you score is XP. XP unlocks new cars.
